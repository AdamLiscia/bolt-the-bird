# Star Trek precons: card audit (2026-11-23)

The 293 unique non-basic cards of the four Star Trek Commander precons (set trc), plus the four commanders,
sorted by what the engine needs to play them. Oracle text: `assets/TRC_oracle.json` (from Scryfall). The tiers come
from an automatic sort (keywords, card type, text length), so some cards will move once they're scripted.
